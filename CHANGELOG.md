# Changelog

All notable changes to *Room for Trouble*. Versions follow `config/version` in
`project.godot`. Protocol and content changes are noted, because mismatched clients cannot
share an online room.

## 2.0.1 — 2026-11-02

- An unplugged controller keeps its button symbols until it's plugged back in or another
  controller takes its seat. Before, prompts rebuilt during that time showed Xbox symbols, and
  the engine logged an error for each rebuilt prompt. Found while measuring on the Steam Deck.
- Measured on a Steam Deck OLED: smoke test passes, and the game holds 90 fps with four players
  on every chapter finale (`docs/PERFORMANCE.md`).

## 2.0.0 — 2026-11-02

The Overcooked-scale rebuild (`docs/DESIGN_V2.md`, decisions D31–D53). Simulation version
2.0.1 and protocol 2: 1.x and 2.0.0 replays are refused, and 1.x clients cannot share a room.

### Game

- New floors: 13×13 four-room chapter 1, 19×13 six-room chapters 2–6, 19×14 split lobby for
  chapter 5; all 24 shifts, the three tutorials, the hub, the Playground and every story scene
  play on them. Characters at twice the scale, a tighter camera, instant take and put, a real
  Quick Step.
- One **order card** per guest on a rail (key, gift, requests, timer), per-order scoring with
  tips for speed, continuous arrivals paced by the rooms, a results screen that says how many
  orders were served, late or missed and when the roster was trimmed.
- The middle step: warm towels and chilled snacks at the heat pump, at an open vent on a warm or
  cold room's edge, or from a helping dragon or yeti; a sprout's watering as a card step;
  festival trunks as job cards showing both handles.
- Lane hazards: steam slows, frost and puddles slide, howls startle; faster shutters; the lift
  has a visible car; the split lobby is dressed with lift signs and a luggage cart.
- Per-chapter light (late morning, noon, dusk, greenhouse, evening, lantern-lit night).

### Story and presentation

- Story scenes stay authored on the classic floor and are staged onto the v2 floors with the
  framing scaled to the toys; a hotel station in the line to a face is hidden for that panel;
  every beat passes the frame check (D48).
- Finished-order cards fade in their last moment instead of lingering as a grey slab.

### Performance

- Pooled particle effects and one mesh per thermostat head: L24 with four players at 1280×800
  renders 120 floor draw calls on Low and 146 on Standard (`docs/PERFORMANCE.md`).

### Readability and accessibility (milestone 4)

- A readability floor for the HUD on a 7" 1280×800 panel: no HUD text under 14 px and no state
  icon under 14 px (seat tags, temper and water badges, card steps, tips, the inspect panel, the
  trunk card's cue); the order rail wraps to a second row at UI scales that leave room for fewer
  than four cards; UI01 checks the rail, clock, objective and portraits at 175%.
- Reduced motion now covers the v2 juice: no Quick Step stretch, a nudge instead of the item
  bounce, half the particles, shorter sound-mark rings.
- Puddles and frost patches carry a dark rim and ripple rings / crystals so they read in every
  colour-vision palette and in high contrast; the lockup and menus follow high contrast.
- Captions and sound marks (a ring with the icon at the source) for slips, startles, the warm /
  chill step at the pump, a vent or a helper, watering, and trunk handles and deliveries.

### Audio (milestone 4)

- New procedural cues in the kit's voice: pass-counter put and take, warm / chill at the pump,
  the vent, trunk grab, slip, startle, order served and order served late (`tools/audio/sfx.py`,
  regenerated byte-identically otherwise).
- Music intensity follows order pressure: a card in the last third of its lobby patience or
  request window lifts the music a step, two lift it to busy, whatever the wave count says.

### Front end (milestone 4)

- A proper title lockup in the pixel style (luggage label, ROOM FOR / TROUBLE bands, family
  bunting, bell, "Version 2" tag) shared by the title, the main menu and the hub menu; the
  title and menu backdrop is the v2 hotel; a "What's new in 2.0" page from the main menu and
  the hub menu.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-23

First complete build of the game described in `docs/DESIGN_BRIEF.md`.

### Game

- 1–4 player couch co-op with per-device seats. Two keyboard slots and any number of
  controllers, each with its own bindings and assists. Drop-in seat lobby.
- Private online rooms: create, or join with an 8-character code; any local/remote seat mix
  up to 4; leader controls; reconnect with a token; per-seat auto-pause.
- The campaign:
  - 3 tutorials and 24 shifts in 6 chapters on 6 floor layouts (P1–P6)
  - an opening, 5 chapter bridges and an ending
  - a pre-shift beat, a live gag and a postcard for every shift
  - progression never depends on stars
- Six guest families with comfort, quirk, helper and visual behaviour. Hotel systems:
  - tickets, gifts, requests, laundry and stock
  - relocation, thermal vents and gates, acoustic curtains
  - moisture, steam and puddles
  - lifts, the shutter, festival trunks and parcels
- Cozy, Family and Busy pace. Gus as a solo helper. A soft wrap-up with a Cozy Corner, and
  one team result with stars and three ribbons per shift.
- The hub hotel, Scrapbook (postcards, guest guide, ribbons, story replays), Wardrobe (8
  cosmetic sets), Quick Shift (8 modifiers, visible seed) and the Playground sandbox.
- Accessibility:
  - remapping and UI scale
  - readable font, high contrast and colour-blind palettes
  - reduced motion, transparency, flashes and camera shake
  - captions with speaker names and sound visualisations
  - mono audio and read-aloud
  - per-seat Auto Catch, toggle Work, Quick Step and Steady Feet

### Presentation

- Menus fit any UI scale: at 175% on a 720p screen, oversized cards shrink to fit instead of
  clipping. The HUD arrival strip never covers the clock, and seat tags never cover prompts.
- The Steam Deck and Steam's Game Mode start fullscreen, using the full 1280×800 panel
  instead of a letterboxed 720p window.
- A local shift pauses itself when the game loses focus (alt-tab, the Steam overlay) or
  the machine wakes from sleep.

- An Overcooked-style fixed perspective camera over a pixel-art 3D scene: low-resolution
  render, integer upscale, toon shading with dithered bands, and an outline pass on Forward+.
- Procedural low-poly actors, stations, items and props, 143 text-authored pixel icons, and
  188 procedurally synthesised music, effect and voice cues.

### Engineering

- Characters are single skinned meshes, station bodies are one merged floor mesh, and only
  main masses cast shadows. The busiest floor dropped from 622 to 154 draw calls (131 on
  the new Low graphics preset).

- A pure, integer, fixed-tick (30 Hz) deterministic simulation shared by local play, the
  online service, tests, the reference bot and replays.
- An authoritative WebSocket room service with strict schema validation, rate limits,
  checkpoints, graceful shutdown and a protocol health probe. Docker and Caddy deployment.
- Versioned, checksummed saves with atomic writes, three backups, migration and idempotent
  results.
- Tests:
  - headless suites with about 2,200 checks
  - controller UI traversal
  - online end-to-end, four-seat and abuse tests
  - restart recovery
  - Linux container smoke tests
  - 126 reference runs
- Protocol version 1. The content hash is printed by the server at startup and by
  `validate_content.gd`.
