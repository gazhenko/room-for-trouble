# Credits

## Room for Trouble

An original cooperative game about running a cozy monster hotel together.

- **Design:** the *Room for Trouble* complete game design and engineering handoff, v1.0
  (2026-10-22), in `docs/DESIGN_BRIEF.md`. It covers the concept, fiction, campaign, level
  scripts and storyboards.
- **Implementation, procedural art, procedural audio, tools and documentation:** built with
  [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) (Anthropic) for the project owner.

## Cast

- **Hosts:** Nana Nook (the warm gargoyle founder), Gus (a smiling suitcase mimic with tiny
  feet), Dot Quill (a small moth festival coordinator with a very big clipboard)
- **Staff:** Pip, Pebble, Moss, Button
- **Guests:** Mallow the Slime, Flurry the Yeti, Cinder the Dragon, Whisp the Ghost, Luna the
  Moonwolf, Fern the Sprout, and their many cousins

## Art and audio

- All 3D models are procedural low-poly meshes authored in code (`src/view/models/`).
- All icons are original pixel art authored as text (`data/art/icons/`).
- All music, stings, sound effects and voice babble are original procedural synthesis
  (`tools/audio/`).
